Desk Assistant

Desk Assistant is a personal tool used only by its owner. It reads the owner's Google Calendar with read-only access and draws the week on a display on the owner's desk. It cannot create, change or delete events.

Privacy policy

Desk Assistant asks Google for one permission: read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It uses the events only to show the owner's own calendar display.

The events and the access key stay on the owner's own computers. They are not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising. Desk Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
